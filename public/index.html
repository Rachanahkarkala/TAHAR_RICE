A little know-how

Good rice, good to know.

A few helpful answers to make every pot of Tahar basmati a little better.

01What makes basmati rice different from other rice?

Basmati is known for its distinctive aroma, slender grains and characteristic length when cooked. Its delicate fragrance and fluffy, separate texture make it a favourite for biryani, pulao and everyday meals.

02Should I wash basmati rice before cooking?

Yes. Rinse the rice gently in cool water two or three times, until the water looks clearer. This removes excess surface starch and helps the cooked grains stay light and separate.

03Do I need to soak basmati rice?

Soaking is helpful, especially for biryani and other dishes where you want long, well-defined grains. After rinsing, soak for around 20–30 minutes, then drain. For everyday cooking, follow the method recommended on your pack.

04What is the best water-to-rice ratio?

It depends on the rice variety, whether it has been soaked, and the cooking method. Start with the instructions on your Tahar pack and adjust slightly for your preferred texture and cookware.

05Which basmati is best for biryani?

Choose a long-grain variety that holds its shape during cooking. Tahar Traditional Basmati and Tahar Premium Basmati are described as suitable for dum cooking and biryani-style dishes.

06How should I store basmati rice?

Keep rice in an airtight container in a cool, dry place, away from moisture, heat and direct sunlight. Always use a clean, dry scoop and close the pack or container after each use.

07Why should cooked basmati rice rest before serving?

A short resting time after cooking lets the moisture settle through the grains. Leave the rice covered for about 5–10 minutes, then gently fluff with a fork before serving.

08How do I choose the right Tahar rice for my kitchen?

Think about the dishes you cook most often, the texture you enjoy and the pack size that suits your household. Explore the Tahar range to find a variety for everyday meals, pulao, biryani and family gatherings.
